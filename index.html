Privacy & Storage Notice

Transparent facts about your task data

100% Client-Side & Private: TaskFlow runs completely on your machine. No accounts, no backend databases, no analytics trackers, and no external AI services are used.

Browser-Specific: Data is saved in this browser's localStorage sandbox. It does not automatically sync across separate browsers, incognito windows, or other devices. To transfer your tasks to another device, use the Export JSON feature above.
